THE CHECKLIST 6 STEPS

What’s happening under the hood.

  1. 01

    Make an account

    Express validates your details and bcrypt hashes your password before saving it.

    ✓
  2. 02

    Get your tokens

    The server signs a short-lived access token and a longer-lived refresh token.

    ✓
  3. 03

    Keep them in cookies

    HTTP-only cookies keep token values out of JavaScript and browser storage.

    ✓
  4. 04

    Visit your profile

    Middleware checks the access token before the protected profile route responds.

    ✓
  5. 05

    Refresh your session

    When the access token expires, the refresh token is rotated and its old copy revoked.

    ✓
  6. 06

    Sign out cleanly

    The refresh token is revoked in SQLite and both authentication cookies are cleared.

    ✓
i

A JWT is signed, not secret. Its header and payload can be read. Never put passwords or sensitive information in a token.
